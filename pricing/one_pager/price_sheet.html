Pricing · 2026

Pricing is based on the size of your corpus.

Two charges: a one-time fee to bring your surveys in, and a flat annual fee based on the size of your corpus. Both are counted in variables. One variable is one substantive column of your data, one question you asked. Nothing is charged per user, per search, or per feature. Starting with a single study carries no annual fee (04).

01Ingestion

We parse each survey from its codebook and data file, then annotate every variable: a readable title, themes and concepts, its response options, and a standard demographic type. That's what makes a question findable across all your studies.

04Proof of concept

No annual fee to start You can start with a single study.

05Terms

  • Prices in Canadian dollars, before applicable taxes, valid through June 30, 2027.
  • Ingestion invoiced per batch on delivery; the annual plan at signature, net 30.
  • Studies are live within 10 business days of receiving your files and codebooks.
  • Demographics count as variables; weights, identifiers and interview metadata don't. Unstructured sources are quoted separately.
  • Annual term, renewed by agreement with 30 days' notice. Your plan changes only at renewal, and only if your corpus size changed.
  • Your data stays in an isolated index, hosted in Canada, and is never used to train models.
